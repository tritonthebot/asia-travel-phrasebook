Money notes

  • Japan & Korea: Very cash-friendly. 7-Eleven ATMs in Japan are reliable.
  • Vietnam: Lots of zeros — ₫100,000 is a common note. “One hundred” often means 100,000.
  • Thailand: Carry small notes for markets & tuk-tuks; PromptPay QR is common.
  • Philippines: GCash is king. Keep ₱20 / ₱50 notes and coins for jeepneys.
  • Indonesia: GoPay / OVO QRIS works almost everywhere; “seratus ribu” = 100,000.

Universal survival kit

  • Screenshot your hotel address in the local script for drivers.
  • Bargain in markets at ~60–70% with a smile — not in malls or fixed-price shops.
  • Factory ice (tube / cylindrical) is generally made from purified water.

How to use

🌏
Country

You pick your country once. It is remembered in this browser on this device, so you will not be asked again. After that it lives in Settings — tap the country name at the top, the gear, or the Settings tab to change it. Every phrase, number and cheat sheet follows your pick.

💬
Phrases

Use the tabs at the bottom: Phrases, Convert, Cheat and Settings. In Phrases, filter with the category pills (Greetings, Essentials, Dining, Transport, Specialized, Numbers) or search in English, local script or romanisation — try “thank you”, “spicy” or “taxi”.

🔊
Listen, Show, Copy, ★

Tap the blue play button or Listen to hear a phrase spoken with native pronunciation. If you are offline it falls back to your device voice. ⛶ shows it full-screen — hold it up to a driver or server. Copy puts it on your clipboard. ★ saves a favourite, remembered in this browser. Set speaking speed and test the voice in Settings › Audio.

🔢
Numbers & money

The Numbers category covers 1–10 plus the big notes you’ll actually hand over — like ¥10,000, ₫100,000 and ₩50,000.

💱
Converter

Rates are fetched live when you open Convert, trying open.er-api.com first, then Frankfurter, then the fawazahmed0 currency-api — the converter shows which source provided your rates. If all three are unreachable (or you’re offline), a labelled built-in fallback estimate is shown instead — tap Refresh live rates to retry, and always check your bank or card rate for the final amount.
